TRAINING LABEL
Estrous-type / Separation / Round-up / Challenge

These remain useful caller-training targets, but they are not presented as unique scientific vocal classes. Use the real elk and expert material above to calibrate tone and delivery.

FIELD CALL CHEAT SHEET
Cow / calf contact

Keep it social and controlled. Practice clean starts, natural pitch movement, and space between sounds.

Distressed calf

Higher urgency and instability than a normal contact call. Use the Distressed Calf target and compare duration/range rather than simply getting louder.

Location bugle

Prioritize a clean rise and sustained high section. Use it to practice locating rather than automatically escalating aggression.

Round-up / bull calling cows

Treat this as a caller-training target. Shorter, raspier herd-directed delivery is different from simply screaming a challenge.

Challenge / aggressive

Practice controlled aggression and structure. Louder alone is not a better score.
